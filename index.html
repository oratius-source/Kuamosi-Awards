Who qualifies for the Kuamosi Award?

The Kuamosi Award is given to a person who embodies the values of the school. The Kitsong values are:

Knowledge Innovation Team Work Sacrality Optimism Nobility Gratitude

We are looking for a learner who embodies a noble character: diligence, punctuality, a strong work ethic, willingness to help others, and a love for people.
